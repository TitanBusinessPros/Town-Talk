📜 How to Play

  • Goal: Capture all territories or eliminate all enemy armies!
  • Setup: Each player starts with 2 swordsmen groups and 1 wizard. 3 hidden bonus squares hold 5 armies each!
  • Units:
    • Swordsmen: Attack adjacent enemies. Higher number wins! (7 vs 5 = 2 remaining)
    • Wizard 🔮: Always 1 unit. Moves 2 squares per turn. Can attack up to 2 squares away in straight lines. Kills 2 units per attack!
    • Archer 🏹 (Stage 5+): Always 1 unit. Attacks up to 3 squares away in straight lines. Kills 1 unit per attack!
  • Your Turn (5 Phases):
    • 🔮 Wizard Movement: Move your wizard up to 2 squares (one square at a time to adjacent empty spaces)
    • 1️⃣ Deploy Phase: Place armies on swordsmen OR expand to adjacent empty squares. Cannot stack armies on wizards or archers!
    • 2️⃣ Expand Phase: Claim adjacent empty squares with swordsmen
    • 3️⃣ Attack Phase: Attack enemies! Wizards shoot magic balls! 🔮⚡ Archers shoot arrows! 🏹
    • 4️⃣ Fortify Phase: Move swordsmen between adjacent territories
    • Bonus Discovery: Find hidden bonus squares - you'll get fireworks and +5 armies! 🎆
    • Trap Doors 🕳️: In some campaign stages, hidden trap doors will destroy units that step on them!
    • Click "Next Phase" to skip optional phases, "End Turn" to finish
  • Strategy:
    • Move your wizard to strategic positions! Use it to snipe enemies from 2 squares away. Protect it - it's fragile!
    • Wizards and archers stay at 1 army - you cannot deploy to them!
    • Use archers for long-range support from safe positions
    • Build defensive formations to protect your special units
  • Campaign Mode: 20 progressively harder stages. Stage 5+ adds archers. Stage 20 is the final boss with multiple wizards and archers!
  • 2 Player Mode / Online: Take turns. No AI — pure strategy! Online play syncs a full turn at a time.
