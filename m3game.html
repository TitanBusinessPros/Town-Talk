🃏 Welcome to Cribbage! 🃏

🎯 Goal: First to 121 Points Wins!

🎴 Setup (Each Round)

  1. Each player gets 6 cards
  2. Both players discard 2 cards into the crib (dealer's bonus hand)
  3. Cut the deck - top card is the starter
  4. If starter is a Jack = Dealer gets 2 points (His Heels)

⚡ Phase 1: THE PLAY (Take Turns Playing Cards)

Players alternate playing one card. Keep count - can't go over 31!

Score Instantly When You:

Make 15→ 2 points
Make 31→ 2 points
Play a pair→ 2 points (6 pts for 3rd, 12 pts for 4th)
Make a run→ 1 point per card (must be 3+ cards in sequence)
Opponent says "Go"→ 1 point (they can't play)

After 31 or "Go", count resets to zero and play continues with remaining cards.

🃏 Phase 2: THE SHOW (Count Your Hand)

Count your 4 cards + the starter card (5 total). Every combo counts!

Any combo = 15→ 2 points each
Pair→ 2 points each pair
Run (3+ in sequence)→ 1 point per card
Flush (all same suit)→ 4 points (5 if starter matches)
Jack same suit as starter→ 1 point (His Nobs)

⚠️ Important: Find ALL combos - a single hand can score multiple ways!

After hands are counted, dealer counts the crib as a bonus hand.

💡 Quick Tips

  • Discard to crib: Dealer = put good cards. Non-dealer = put bad cards (A, K)
  • Safe lead: Play a 4. Avoid leading 5 (opponent makes 15 easily)
  • Avoid 21: Don't make count 21 (opponent plays 10-card for 31)

Card Values: A=1, 2-10=face value, J/Q/K=10
